About Preclinical Drug Development

Determining a drug's safety profile is one of the most critical stages of the drug development pathway. Preclinical safety assessment studies are essential for evaluating the potential risks of new therapeutic compounds before they can progress to human trials. Complications and inefficiencies with preclinical studies can create costly delays in bringing life-saving medications to patients. Charles River Laboratories is a leading Contract Research Organization (CRO) that supports pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies through comprehensive preclinical testing services using state-of-the-art technologies in a global network of facilities.

About the Service

The Formulation Services Department at Charles River's Elphinstone facility in Tranent (near Edinburgh) is responsible for preparing, characterizing, and managing test items used in preclinical safety assessment studies. We support both GLP (Good Laboratory Practice) and non-GLP studies, working with diverse compounds and formulation types.

Key Functions

Formulation Preparation: We prepare vehicles and formulations using a variety of techniques tailored to study requirements. This includes precise mixing, homogenization, and quality control to ensure consistency and stability of test substances. Each formulation is prepared according to detailed Formulation Process Documents and Good Laboratory Practice standards.

Test Item Management: We maintain secure storage and strict control of test items throughout the preclinical study lifecycle. This includes proper labelling, traceability, and documentation to ensure the integrity of the test substance. All records are maintained in accordance with ALCOA+ principles (Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate, and supported by digital infrastructure).

Sampling and Analysis: Archive samples and formulation analysis samples are systematically collected and preserved. These samples are analysed to verify formulation specifications, confirm homogeneity, and document stability. Regular testing ensures formulations meet study requirements throughout the study duration.

Quality Assurance: All work is conducted in accordance with Study Plans (Protocols), Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and the principles of Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) and Good Clinical Practice (GCP). We maintain meticulous records and collaborate closely with other departments to ensure study integrity and regulatory compliance.
